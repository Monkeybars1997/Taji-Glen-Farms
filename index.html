Great agriculture isn't just about volume; it's about attention to detail. Founded in the fertile soils of Mwea, we believe that the highest quality crops require a bespoke approach.

As the industry rushes toward heavy machinery, it often sacrifices quality for quantity. We rely on dedicated, hand-cultivated care to ensure maximum leaf integrity and plant health guaranteeing you a premium product every time.

Our 'hand picked' approach is a deliberate quality choice allowing for highly selective harvesting which ensures that the harvest of each plant happens at its peak potency.

  • No mechanical leaf bruising during harvest
  • Specific pruning per plant requirements
  • Integrated pest management via human scouting

The Farm-to-Soil Cycle

In light of Kenya's commitment to achieving net zero by 2050, we have decided to get ahead of the curve by placing sustainability at the forefront of our values. We have integrated a closed-loop, regenerative agricultural system designed to naturally sustain our crops and eliminate synthetic reliance.

🌾

1. Maize Silage

A dedicated section of our farm grows high-quality maize specifically as feed.

🐑

2. Livestock

Our sheep consume the farm-grown fodder, acting as natural processors.

🌱

3. Natural Fertilizer

The livestock produces premium organic waste, applied directly back to our crops.
